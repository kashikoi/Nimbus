Welcome aboard

A calm weekly command deck for your real life.

Nimbus is a local-first planner built around one practical rhythm: catch actions quickly, schedule them into your Saturday-through-Friday week, and keep flexible groups for anything that does not belong on the calendar yet. No accounts, no sync, no ceremony.

  1. 1

    Capture an action fast

    Use Add Action to drop a blank action into the top list. It starts ready to type, so you can capture the thought first and decide where it belongs later.

    Add Action
    Add Group
  2. 2

    Split your world into tabs

    The tab bar under the header holds separate boards. This Week is the default tab and the only one with the Saturday-through-Friday week columns; click + to add another tab for a project or focus list with its own actions and custom groups. Click a tab's name to rename it, and use its × to delete it (This Week cannot be deleted).

    Deleting a tab removes its actions and custom groups too, so move anything you want to keep first.

  3. 3

    Edit where the words already are

    Actions look like finished cards, but the text itself is editable. Click the words, type the update, then press Enter or click away to save.

    Blank actions show a soft New action placeholder until you name them.

  4. 4

    Build a Saturday-first week

    The week always starts with Saturday and runs through Friday. Each day shows the current date on the right, and today gets a clear accent highlight.

    SaturdayAug 29
    TuesdaySep 1
  5. 5

    Create your own holding areas

    Use Add Group to create a blank custom group below the week. Name it inline, then drag actions into it for project buckets, errands, waiting-for items, or anything you want to save outside the weekly plan. Groups belong to the tab they were created in, so each board keeps its own set.

  6. 6

    Drag things until they feel right

    Drag actions from To-Dos into a day or custom group. Drag within a list to reorder. Nearby cards glide out of the way as you sort, and holding a dragged card near the top or bottom edge scrolls the page for you.

    Custom group headers are draggable too, so your personal sections can be rearranged below the week.

  7. 7

    Check off, clean up, or remove a group

    The round control on each card marks it done. The trash icon removes an action. Custom groups have their own trash icon; when you delete one, Nimbus asks whether to move its actions back to To-Dos or delete those actions with the group.

  8. 8

    Try a realistic sample week

    Open Settings and use Randomize data (demo) to load a lived-in sample across three tabs: This Week (day cards, done items, a custom group), Side Projects, and Someday, each with their own actions and groups. It replaces every existing tab, so export first if you want to keep yours.

  9. 9

    Settings, backup, and atmosphere

    Settings holds the atmosphere picker, export/import, demo data, and clear-data control. Export saves every tab, its actions and custom groups, done states, order, and theme into one JSON file. Import restores all of it and replaces what is currently on this device; Clear all data wipes tasks, groups, and tabs back to a blank This Week.

One important thing

Nimbus stores your planner only in this browser on this device. That keeps it private and simple, but it also means browser-data cleanup can erase it. Export a backup from Settings when your planner starts to matter.
